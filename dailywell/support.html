Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation or reset email.
Check your spam folder, wait a few minutes, then try again. Make sure you typed the address correctly. Opening the link on your phone opens a web page; after it says you are confirmed, return to the app and log in.
How do I reset my password?
On the login screen tap "Forgot your password?", enter your email, and open the link we send. Choose a new password on the page that opens, then log in to the app.
My reminders don't arrive.
Open the Reminders page and tap "Turn on notifications", then allow notifications for DailyWell in your phone's Settings. On Android, also check that battery optimisation is not restricting DailyWell and that Do Not Disturb is off. Reminders are scheduled for the next 7 days each time you open the app, so open it at least once a week. Quiet hours (Settings) silence reminders in that time window.
How do I export my data?
Settings → "Download everything (JSON)", or Reports → "Export my data (CSV)".
How do I delete my account?
Settings → "Delete my account and data", type DELETE to confirm. This permanently erases your account and all entries and cannot be undone. If you can't open the app, email me from the address on your account and I will do it.
Is DailyWell medical advice?
No. It is a tracker and reminder tool. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health, diet, exercise and medicines. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
