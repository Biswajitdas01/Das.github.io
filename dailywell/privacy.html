DailyWell

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

DailyWell (the "app") is a personal health routine tracker made by Biswajit Chandra Das ("I", "me"), based in Los Angeles, California, USA. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: biswajitchandrad4@gmail.com.

In short: you type in your own health-routine information; it is stored in your private account so you can use it on your devices; I do not sell it, show ads, run analytics or ad trackers, or send it to AI services. You can export or permanently delete everything from inside the app.

Information the app handles

WhatWhy
Account: email address, password (stored only as a salted hash by the authentication service), optional display nameTo create your account, sign you in, and send confirmation or password-reset emails
Health and fitness entries you type in: workouts, exercises, meals and nutrition numbers, water, sleep times, recovery sessions, soreness, supplements or medicines you choose to track, body measurements (weight, body fat, waist and other measurements, resting heart rate, blood pressure), habits, mood/energy/stress check-ins and notesTo provide the features of the app: tracking, charts, reports, insights and reminders
Settings and goals: units, targets, bedtime and wake time, optional age, sex, height and activity level (used only by the optional calorie calculator), reminder preferencesTo personalise the app for you
Identifiers: an internal account IDTo keep your records separate from everyone else's

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifier, or browsing history. It does not read data from Apple Health, Google Health Connect or any wearable.

Where your information is stored

Your account and entries are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, my service provider for authentication and data storage. Data is protected in transit with HTTPS/TLS and is protected by row-level security rules so that your account can read and change only your own records. Some app data and your sign-in session are also kept on your device.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone using the operating system's local notifications. No push-notification server is used. A reminder's text (for example "Take Vitamin D") can appear on your lock screen, so turn reminders off or change their wording if that concerns you.

How information is used and shared

  • It is used only to run the app for you.
  • I do not sell your information or share it for advertising, and the app has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • I do not send your entries to artificial-intelligence services.
  • Service providers (Supabase, and the email service used to deliver account emails) process data only to provide their service to me.
  • I may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety or rights.
  • As the operator I have technical administrative access to the database. I do not look at individual users' entries except where needed to fix a technical problem or where the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → "Download everything (JSON)", or Reports → CSV export.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete: Settings → "Delete my account and data" permanently erases your account and all your entries immediately. You can also email me and I will do it for you. Backups made by the hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app (Reminders page) or in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California) you may have additional rights, such as objecting to processing or lodging a complaint with a regulator. Email me to exercise any right.

Retention

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from the live database.

Children

DailyWell is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and I do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I will delete it.

International users

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where my service providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws than your own.

Not medical advice

DailyWell is a tracker and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health, diet, exercise, supplements and medicines.

Security

I use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no online service is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy I will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
