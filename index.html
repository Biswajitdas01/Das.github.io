I am Biswajit Chandra Das — an AI & Data Analytics enthusiast whose curiosity about how machines learn has evolved into a deep passion for research, innovation, and responsible technology.

My journey sits at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, and Generative AI. I don't just study technology — I interrogate it, question it, and ask how it can be shaped to serve humanity better.

Whether it's building models that detect financial fraud, analyzing scam patterns in cryptocurrency ecosystems, or exploring how AI fortifies digital defenses, my work is driven by one core belief: data, when understood deeply, becomes a force for good.

I approach every problem with a researcher's rigor and a builder's restlessness — continuously learning, persistently experimenting, and never satisfied with surface-level answers.
